## Sets of Electrostatics and Electrodynamics
The idea of making sets are simple, because it's really really hard to maintain big latex files, because it
can take a lots of time debugging things, and big latex-pdf document can look more like books than the problem sets, so I decide to break those down the chunks, covering all the things, and whole syllabus like this!
The Plan is making each sets contain 10-15 questions, rather than everything! Let's test with this one!
